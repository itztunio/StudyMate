About ChatBond AI

"Your chats, your memories, your insights."

🎯 Project Mission

ChatBond AI is an intuitive, full-stack web application developed to help friends, couples, and groups uncover meaningful, fun patterns in their messaging habits. By analyzing standard exported WhatsApp conversation archives, users can appreciate active moments, shared laughs, and conversational balance.

🛡️ Privacy & Security First

  • Temporary Memory Processing: Uploaded chat files are processed strictly in-memory or in ephemeral temporary buffers during your session. They are never committed to permanent databases or persistent storage.
  • Zero Account Linking: ChatBond AI works entirely with standard exported .txt files and requires no access to your WhatsApp account or QR codes.
  • Google Gemini AI Handling: Full conversations are never transmitted to AI models. Only messages you deliberately type or paste into the AI Reply Generator are transmitted to Google Gemini to formulate suggestions.
  • Clear On Demand: You can purge your session metrics at any time by clicking "Analyze Another Chat" or clearing your browser session.

⚙️ Technology Stack

Backend: Python Flask 3.x
Frontend: Modern HTML5, Responsive CSS3, JavaScript ES6
Visualizations: Chart.js 4.x
AI Integration: Google GenAI SDK (Gemini)

⚠️ Entertainment & Ethics Disclaimer

The Friendship Score and trivia metrics are calculated from basic structural communication data (such as frequency, active days, and emoji density) solely for entertainment purposes. They do not constitute psychological assessments, loyalty indicators, or proof of emotional attachment.
